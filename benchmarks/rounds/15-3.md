  round 3's row-4 score stands as a CRITIQUE and NOT as a blind comparison.**
  The method says the critic "is never told which is ours". It was not told.
  But the method's *purpose* is that it cannot know, and identity was
  inferable from its context before it saw a pixel. Told and cannot-know are
  different, and the difference is the entire value of the blind leg. A round
  file reporting this as a blind comparison would be claiming a property the
  round did not have — the exact failure `benchmarks/README.md` exists to
  prevent, and the one CLAUDE.md's remaining-work item 42 already records
