  says the critic gets four things, and the reference's credential dialog
  is the surface plan 01's picker replaces.

  16 are scored "against test output", and the ledger wants `ours` / `ref`

  `flowiseai/flowise:latest` is 2.2.4 (built 2025-01-14), not 3.1.4;
